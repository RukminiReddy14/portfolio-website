Projects

Game-Day Forecasting for Concessions

Tableau ↗
Problem
Concessions teams have to plan staff and inventory weeks before any sales data comes in.
Data
20 years of home games (1,647) combined with NOAA weather.
Insight
Three factors known in advance drive a nearly 3× swing in attendance, from about 9.8K to 26.4K fans.
Solution
A Low / Medium / High demand-tier model and a Tableau dashboard.
Impact
Planners can schedule staff and inventory 2–3 weeks ahead.

F1 Race Summarizer and Predictor

GitHub ↗
Problem
Turning raw F1 race data into a clear race story takes hours of manual analysis.
Data
Lap-by-lap telemetry for 20+ drivers across every race from 2018 to now (FastF1, Ergast).
Engine
Rule-based detection of 5 race event types with 83% accuracy on noisy data.
AI layer
Feeds the detected events to Claude or GPT through a compact prompt (under 2,000 tokens), so the report sticks to facts.
Impact
A full race report in under 20 seconds, generated with one click in a Streamlit app.

AskRC: RAG Chatbot for Research Computing

Problem
Research Computing information is spread across many pages, making answers hard to find.
Pipeline
Daily Airflow pipeline scrapes and indexes the information into Azure AI Search (~2,700 chunks).
AI layer
GPT-4 answers only from retrieved information, with bias and relevance checks on every answer.
MLOps
Automated testing and Azure deployment via GitHub Actions, with MLflow logging and Slack alerts.
Impact
Grounded answers in one place, refreshed automatically every day.
